# CHH pilot legal-counsel checklist

Internal working document. Do not render or publish this checklist on the public
website. It records decisions for qualified counsel and the applicable school
agreements; it is not a claim of legal compliance.

## Parties and agreements

- Confirm the contracting legal entity, trading name, registered address and
  authorised signatories before issuing school agreements.
- Confirm the pilot agreement, end date, renewal or conversion path, termination,
  offboarding and return/export arrangements.
- Define commercial terms, invoicing, taxes, refunds and any school-specific
  implementation or support commitments.

## Privacy and student information

- Confirm the responsibilities of each participating school and the service for
  school, staff, student, guardian, survey, message, media and safeguarding data.
- Identify the applicable countries and legal frameworks for each pilot school,
  including any child, education, privacy, employment or communications rules.
- Confirm the lawful basis, notices, permissions or consent records needed for
  staff accounts, student records, guardian links, messaging, photos, surveys and
  family delivery.
- Approve the data-processing agreement, necessary provider list, processing
  locations and any international-transfer wording or safeguards.
- Approve a record-by-record retention schedule, including backups, audit history,
  messaging, media, surveys, behaviour, recognition and safeguarding material.
- Confirm the routes, identity checks, response periods and school/service division
  for access, correction, restriction, objection, portability and deletion
  requests.
- Confirm incident and breach-assessment, notification and school-coordination
  duties.

## Service terms and risk

- Approve the acceptable-use, suspension and account-termination provisions.
- Decide the warranties, exclusions, liability limits, indemnities and remedies
  appropriate for the pilot and each target country.
- Define any availability, maintenance, support-response, recovery, backup or
  service-credit commitments rather than inferring them from engineering targets.
- Confirm intellectual-property ownership and licences for school-entered content,
  product materials, feedback and demonstration assets.
- Confirm the governing law, dispute route and any mandatory local consumer,
  education or public-sector terms.

## Final review

- Review the English Privacy Policy and Terms of Service against the signed school
  agreement and current technical operation.
- Obtain an independent Arabic legal-language review based on the approved English
  meaning.
- Recheck every public claim about security, hosting, retention, providers,
  certification and service availability before broader launch.
